Create original illustrative retro-fantasy game art informed by 1970s and
1980s painted fantasy paperbacks and pre-digital game-cover illustration.
Evoke the imagined worlds behind old games, not the technology that limited
their screens. No pixel art, deliberate pixelation, low-resolution sprites,
photorealism, glossy PBR rendering, or modern cinematic concept-art finish.

Use clear silhouettes, bold local color, deliberate shape design, slightly
eccentric asymmetry and recognizable workmanship. Suggest early-modern
fantasy through useful construction, work clothing, hand tools, practical
storage and gunpowder-era equipment when relevant to the subject. Do not add
medieval armor, steampunk machinery or modern tactical gear by default.

Starting palette: warm ivory and ochre, sun-faded rust/madder red, softened
teal/olive, dark wood and iron. Maintain distinct colors and useful value
separation; avoid a uniform sepia wash. Serious, strange and lived-in, with
warmth as well as danger; neither generic grimdark nor broad cartoon comedy.

Use a few large readable forms and restrained descriptive detail. Keep
painted-print character selective and subordinate to structure. Avoid dense
surface noise, photographic grain, ubiquitous scratches, fabric micro-weave,
excess pouches and straps, faux aged-paper overlays and decorative borders.
