Asset contract: one isolated clutter or interactable object, or the explicitly
requested compact grouping, for a world billboard in a first-person dungeon.
Use a level, near-orthographic view from the specified direction with restrained
perspective. Keep the complete silhouette and clear margins. Show supporting
feet/base if freestanding; specify a consistent mounting plane if wall-mounted.

Give the object a readable function and a strong interaction focal point.
Avoid a dense pile of tiny accessories. Surface marks express construction and
use rather than indiscriminate dirt. No surrounding architecture, floor slab,
labels, interface icon frame, selection halo or additional unrelated objects.

Genuinely transparent background, crisp alpha edges, no matte/checkerboard,
vignette, detached glow or environmental cast shadow. Neutral diffuse light,
clear local colors, restrained intrinsic shading; dynamic level lights provide
changing light. No generated normal map. If the subject is a lamp, restrict any
painted emission to the lamp itself; do not paint its illumination on a scene.
