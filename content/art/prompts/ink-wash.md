Treatment: precise fantasy drawing with painted color. Let expressive contour
and shape carry the structure. Use selective ink-like outer edges, fine
internal lines and sparse intentional hatching to describe folds, anatomy and
changes of plane. Do not hatch every surface or fill quiet shapes with texture.

Favor tapering forms, elegant irregular hems, organic curves, distinctive
negative spaces and restrained decorative asymmetry. Keep believable weight
and construction rather than making every figure elongated in the same way.

Color is broad matte or translucent painted wash over the drawing. Preserve
quiet areas and crisp color separation. Structural illustration can be rich
while material detail remains low. Keep line weights readable after reduction.
Use modest form shading; do not add dramatic illumination contrary to the
asset's light-ready contract.
