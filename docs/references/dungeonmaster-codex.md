# Dungeon Master Codex: source map

Donor root: `/home/research/blobber-games/dungeonMaster-codex`. Its `README.md` describes the local `0.9.4` React/TypeScript/Vite/React Three Fiber remake: grid exploration, inventory/drag/drop, creatures, melee/ranged combat, projectiles, spells, survival, persistence, and endgame.

Use this as behavioral evidence for Rifles. Preserve Rifles' C#/Engine ownership; do not copy donor code or runtime topology.

## Eight useful call paths

1. **Typed spatial and item contracts.** `CreatureCell`, `CreatureInstance`, `FloorItem`, and `ChampionEquipment` live in `src/types/game.ts:228-273`. A creature has a tile plus `center/frontLeft/frontRight/backLeft/backRight`, optional `groupId`, HP/alive state, and carried items. A floor item has stable id, map position, charges, champion-bone link, projectile-drop flag, and recursive container contents. `Projectile` and persisted collections are in `src/engine/runtimeTypes.ts:78-132,211-269`.

2. **Same-tile occupancy and capacity.** `src/engine/systems/storeCreatureSpatialRuntime.ts:82-133` wires `canCreatureShareTile` to `src/engine/systems/runtimeGroupOccupancy.ts:11-44`. Occupants must be alive, same type, same runtime group, and below capacity derived from `sizeOnTile`; different groups cannot merge. `src/engine/systems/creatureTileState.ts:14-64,88-101` normalizes front/back subcells and guards one cell. This is the useful model for several same-family enemies sharing one grid square with readable offsets.

3. **Group movement and AI on a real-time tick.** `src/engine/systems/monsterTickRuntime.ts:144-180,185-220,269-314` iterates alive current-level creatures, creates one `groupMovementPlans` map, and feeds each through `resolveMonsterSingleTurn`. That turn resolves movement before attack using post-move position (`src/engine/systems/monsterSingleTurn.ts:223-335`). `src/engine/systems/monsterMovementTurn.ts:59-154` gates a per-creature move timer, caches one result per `groupId|level|x|y`, and supports ranged spacing via `src/engine/systems/creatureMovementState.ts:40-82`.

4. **Party grid movement and timing.** `src/engine/systems/partyMoveCommand.ts:76-173` rejects input outside exploration or while `movementCooldown > 0`, computes a cardinal target from party facing, checks creature blocking/walkability, and routes a valid step through transport. `src/engine/systems/partyMovementCooldownState.ts:33-103` takes the slowest living champion's load, stamina, wounds, and equipment bonuses. `src/engine/systems/tickFrameState.ts:80-172` sequences regen, movement cooldown, combat, pending sensors/generators, and immediate transport.

5. **Front attack, contact cells, and ranged AI.** `src/engine/systems/monsterAttackTurn.ts:102-209,222-334` computes post-move distance and lane alignment, asks `src/engine/systems/creatureAttackOpportunity.ts:26-65` whether melee or ranged attack is legal, then advances a back cell into `frontLeft/frontRight`, launches a projectile, steals, or resolves damage. `src/engine/systems/frontCreatureState.ts:92-128,159-216` selects exposed champions from party facing and creature cell, preserving directional blobber targeting.

6. **Physical throw/shoot ownership and traversal.** `src/engine/systems/attackFrontRuntime.ts:137-167,200-261` selects the equipped attack and delegates throw/shoot to `buildPhysicalProjectileAttackPatch`. `src/engine/systems/attackPhysicalState.ts:82-170` consumes the thrown hand item or compatible ammo; `src/engine/systems/attackPhysicalProjectiles.ts:68-156` records range, decay, and `physicalItem`. `src/engine/systems/spellProjectileTickRuntime.ts:259-430` advances all projectiles through `src/engine/systems/projectileTraversal.ts:76-214` and `src/engine/systems/projectileContinuation.ts:16-107`, dropping recoverable physical items on walls/misses.

7. **Spell preparation, launch, and impact.** `src/engine/systems/castSpellRuntime.ts:53-75` resolves rune ids and delegates to `src/engine/systems/spellCastState.ts:35-82`, which distinguishes blocked, failed, non-projectile, and projectile casts. `src/engine/systems/spellCastPreparation.ts:62-135` applies recovery, skill check, and XP; `src/engine/systems/spellProjectileCasting.ts:46-93` starts at the party tile in party facing and records effect/range/decay. Shared impacts are `src/engine/systems/tickProjectileCreatureHit.ts:86-131` and `src/engine/systems/tickProjectilePartyHit.ts:168-218`.

8. **Inventory ownership and drag/drop.** `src/engine/systems/inventoryState.ts:24-49,111-135,154-241,275-325,422-489` makes `locateChampionItem` search inventory/equipment and enforces 17 carried items, 8 container contents, displacement, and atomic give/drop/throw patches; `throwChampionCarriedItem` removes the source before appending its projectile. `src/components/UI/dragPayload.ts:4-58` identifies item, champion, slot, and container; `src/components/Dungeon/dungeonDragDrop.ts:1-70` maps pointer bands to `current/front/throw`, and `src/components/Dungeon/DungeonScene.tsx:1438-1478` invokes store actions. `src/components/Dungeon/FloorItemMesh.tsx:53-103,149-210` is presentation only, including occupied-tile lift/stacking and click/drag.

## Limits and search vocabulary

The README says the broad systems above are present; open work is UX, cross-browser end-screen verification, and profiling only when a symptom appears (`README.md:24-42`). The donor's fidelity matrix is the stronger caveat: group capacity/lifecycle is an approximation, not literal FTL `ACTIVE_GROUP`; internal fields and edge-case rehydration rules remain absent (`docs/FIDELITY_REMAINING_MATRIX.md:69-88`). `Throw/Shoot` consumes source-backed descriptors, but its launch layer is partly modeled rather than a complete `Attack.cpp` mirror (`docs/FIDELITY_REMAINING_MATRIX.md:162-173`).

`runAttackFrontRuntime` has an explicit `originalActionNotImplemented` branch for unsupported utility attacks (`src/engine/systems/attackFrontRuntime.ts:264-284`), so a filename or enum is not proof of a finished ability. The audit records no reliable target-game implementation evidence for Ruster rust or generic Immobilize, and limits special teleport evidence to Lord Chaos (`docs/RUNTIME_FIDELITY_AUDIT.md:194-201`).
