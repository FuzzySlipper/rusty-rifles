Treatment: tight pre-digital commercial fantasy painting with hard-edged
illustrative realism. Define forms with precise painted boundaries, controlled
volume and smooth restrained gradients. Use naturalistic anatomy and orderly
construction. Visible outline and brushwork are minimal; value and color
changes carry most edges.

Keep convincing weight and recognizable cloth, wood, leather and iron without
photographic microtexture, glossy PBR highlights or uniformly intense detail.
Use a few readable costume and equipment masses. Detail should support the
silhouette instead of filling it with accessories.

Preserve painted local color and modest form shading, but reduce deep baked
shadows, rim lights and bright directional highlights for runtime sprites.
Do not interpret 'cover painting' as permission to add a background scene,
spotlight, vignette, title or frame against the asset contract.
